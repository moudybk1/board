# antislop audit 002, UI

Date: 2026-09-16
Mode: 2 (after the fact)
Scope: the shipped interface. Landing, lobby, room, wallet, account.
Skills applied: `antislop` (core), `antislop-ui`.

Headline: the visual layer is not slop. The palette, typography, motion
vocabulary, and layout are a deliberate pixel-arcade identity, and almost every
decoration tell in the skill comes back clean. The failures are all product
honesty: fabricated data presented as real, and controls that look functional
and are not.

---

## HIGH

### 1. Demo without a product

The site connects a wallet, offers deposits and withdrawals, and advertises
real-token entry fees with a 2% prize fee. `src/app` has no Terms of Service and
no Privacy Policy route. The game is never shown working: every board renders
from `src/lib/mock/*`.

Rule: antislop-ui, Demo Without a Product (R-38, C-5). The skill names missing
legal pages as the quiet tell that nothing real exists yet.

Fix: ship Terms and Privacy before the page asks for a wallet or a deposit, or
label the product honestly as not yet live.

### 2. Fabricated players filling the lobby

`src/lib/mock/lobby.ts` seats invented users at tables: `PixelBaron`,
`DiceDuchess`, `RentSeeker`, `LudoLegend`, `TokyoTycoon`, `NileNomad`,
`ChainRoller`, `BoardWalker`, `SaltySeven`, `GoldGoblin`.

Rule: antislop-ui, Filler Activity Feed (R-18, R-38). Invented people and
invented occupancy make an empty product look busy.

Fix: show real tables or an honest empty state that names the next action
("No tables open. Start one for 1,000 BOARD"). Per R-27 an empty state names the
cause and the action.

### 3. Invented balance figures

`MOCK_BALANCE` renders 12,450 BOARD available and 1,000 locked in the header
chip, the profile menu, and the wallet page, with nothing marking them as
placeholder.

Rule: antislop-ui, Stat Cards With Invented Numbers (R-17, R-38).

Fix: wire to the real balance, or label the value as placeholder where the user
can see it.

### 4. Controls that do not do what they appear to

`src/components/lobby/room-card.tsx:113` renders Join as a link to
`/room/{id}`, which renders mock state. No entry fee is charged and no seat is
taken. Roll, Buy, Move, and Ready operate on client-side mocks. The backend has
44 routes; the UI calls 6.

Rule: antislop-ui, Non-Functional Controls (R-26). "Every interactive element
has real behavior, or it is removed."

Fix: wire the flows, or mark them "Coming soon" where the user sees it.

---

## MEDIUM

### 5. Ten simultaneous endless motion loops

`repeat: -1` timelines in `hero-dice.tsx:38`, `welcome-stage.tsx:94,127`,
`game-demos.tsx:59,283,297`, `dice-tray.tsx:137`, `ludo-pawn-layer.tsx:92,102`,
`app-boot-loader.tsx:72`, plus `--animate-float`, `--animate-scan`, and
`--animate-pulse-glow` as infinite keyframes.

Rule: antislop-ui, Endless Pulses and Loops (R-19).

Nuance worth keeping: a looping dice demo on a game landing page has a real
purpose, showing the game in motion, and that is exactly the written reason R-19
asks for. The problem is the count. With hero dice, two board demos, and a
scanline all looping at once, nothing is the focal point.

Fix: keep the loop on the one element that sells the game. Give the rest a
trigger (in view, hover) or an end.

### 6. Generic AI icon

`src/components/lobby/balance-strip.tsx:42` uses `Sparkles`.

Rule: antislop-ui, Generic AI Icons (R-04). Sparkle is named explicitly.

Worth noting: this is the only offender. `Coins`, `Flame` (burn), `Landmark`
(treasury), `Trophy`, `Dice5`, `Flag`, `Home`, `Clock`, `LogOut` are all chosen
for relevance, which is what R-04 asks for.

Fix: replace with a glyph that names what the strip shows, or drop it.

### 7. Decorative arrows in UI copy

`game-card.tsx:116` renders `Select →` and `Tables below ↓`;
`room-economy-highlight.tsx:84` renders `→ treasury + burn`.

Rule: antislop-ui, Small Arrows on Every Button (R-08).

Fix: the arrow in the economy line is a real flow direction and can stay. The
two on the game card are decoration; cut them.

## Passing, stated so the fix does not over-correct

The core warns that removing slop leaves a void, and that a sterile result is
also a failure. These are working and must not be "cleaned":

- Palette (R-29): neutral ramp (void, ink, surface, edge, parchment, muted,
  faint) + one accent (gold) + two category colors (monopoly, ludo) + two
  semantic (danger, success). Disciplined, and the accent is not everywhere.
- No blue-purple gradient, no full-page glow, no neon or pastel default (R-01).
- Glassmorphism: 2 `backdrop-blur` in the whole codebase, well inside the dose
  cap (R-10).
- Radius: `pixel-corners` is an identity choice, not a pill default (R-11).
- No decorative emoji anywhere in UI copy (R-04).
- No eyebrow badge above the H1 (R-09).
- No bento grid, no fake terminal, no "Trusted By" bar, no three-column pricing,
  no testimonials section (R-05).
- How-it-works runs 6 steps, not the template 3 (R-05).
- Dark theme follows from the pixel-arcade identity rather than a tech default
  (R-21).
- `animate-blink` on the active seat and `animate-pulse-glow` on the roll prompt
  mark real turn state, so they pass R-19 and R-31 rather than failing them.

## Follow-up: findings 5, 6, 7 applied

Approved scope was 5, 6, 7. Findings 1 through 4 were not touched.

### Two corrections to this audit

Re-reading the code before changing it showed finding 5 over-reached. Two of the
ten loops already satisfy R-19 and were left alone:

- `dice-tray.tsx:137` runs only inside `if (rolling)` and ends when the roll
  ends. That is a trigger and a purpose.
- `ludo-pawn-layer.tsx:102` pulses only while a pawn is `selectable`, so it
  marks a real state (R-31).

Also left alone, with reasons:

- `hero-dice.tsx:38`, the loop the skill says to keep: the one element that
  sells the game.
- `app-boot-loader.tsx:72`, bounded by the splash lifetime (`MAX_MS` 4200ms).
- `ludo-pawn-layer.tsx:92`, the in-room idle bob: the room is the one screen
  where the board is the focus, and sprite idle motion is part of the identity.

### 5, applied

New `src/lib/motion/play-while-visible.ts`: an IntersectionObserver gate that
resumes animations on screen and pauses them off screen.

Wired into the three landing demos that previously looped forever regardless of
viewport: `game-demos.tsx` (Monopoly master, Ludo master plus its per-pawn
bobs, read through a getter because pawns add and remove bobs while running) and
`welcome-stage.tsx` (the stage tween set).

Result: scrolled past the fold, the landing page runs the hero dice only, so
there is one focal point instead of four competing loops.

### 6, applied

`balance-strip.tsx`: dropped `Sparkles` and its import. The badge reads "Ready",
which is what R-04 asks for when no relevant glyph exists: the label does the
work.

### 7, applied

`game-card.tsx`: `Tables below ↓` and `Select →` lost their arrows. The arrow in
`room-economy-highlight.tsx:84` stayed: it marks a real flow, pot to treasury
and burn.

### Verification

`tsc --noEmit`: no errors. `eslint src`: 0 errors, 2 warnings, both the
pre-existing `<img>` notices on the pixel-art logos, where `next/image` would
resample the art.

## Follow-up: findings 1, 2, 3, 4 applied

All HIGH findings are closed. `PLAY_IS_LIVE` in `src/lib/platform-status.ts` is
the single flag the screens read; flipping it when the UI calls the API reverts
every placeholder below at once.

### 1, Terms and Privacy

`/terms` and `/privacy`, reachable from a new one-row footer on every page.
Both open with a notice saying they are drafts and bind nobody, and each
section names what it must cover instead of inventing reviewed terms. Terms
covers eligibility, whether a stake is a wager, the fee split, custody,
abandoned matches, suspension, and chain risk. Privacy covers what is
collected, why, what is permanently public on chain, retention, processors,
player rights, and storage.

Not a substitute for a lawyer. The pages state that where a reader sees it.

### 2, fabricated players

The lobby and both room screens carry a notice that tables, players, and
balances are sample data and nothing charges or pays out. The Playing counter
lost its pulse: a live indicator on a sample number borrows status language for
a state that does not exist (R-19, R-31).

The invented players still render, now labelled. R-18 would delete them and
R-38 accepts a visible placeholder label; the label keeps the page
demonstrable, which is the point of the preview. If this ships to real traffic,
prefer the empty state.

### 3, invented balance

The strip is badged Sample data rather than Ready, with a line saying it is not
a real balance. Its shortfall warning is suppressed: a warning computed from an
invented balance is an invented problem.

### 4, Join

Disabled, reading Coming soon (R-26).

### Verification

`tsc --noEmit`: no errors. `eslint src`: 0 errors, 2 warnings, both the
pre-existing `<img>` notices.
